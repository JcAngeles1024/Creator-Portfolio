Anything you drop in this folder appears in the moving strip on the home page.

Just paste the file in. No code to edit.

  Images   .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .avif .gif
  Videos   .mp4 .mov .webm .m4v

Optional, if you want more control:

  Order        Files appear in filename order. Put 01- 02- 03- in front
               of the names to arrange them.

  Caption      The filename becomes the description read out by screen
               readers, so "behind-the-scenes.jpg" reads as
               "Behind the scenes".

  Video start  Videos play a 5 second loop from the beginning. To start
               somewhere else, put @ and the second in the name:
               "reel@12s.mp4" plays from 0:12 to 0:17.

  Video cover  Give an image the same name as the video and it becomes
               the still shown before the video starts:
               "reel.mp4" and "reel.jpg".

Portrait and landscape both work. Tiles are all the same height and take
their width from the shape of the file, so nothing gets cropped.

Keep videos short and small - a few MB. A large camera export will be slow
to load.
